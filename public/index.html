About

About Acharya

Acharya is a premier educational community based in Bangalore, known for its commitment to innovation, excellence, and holistic student development. With a legacy of over three decades, Acharya offers a diverse and dynamic learning environment that nurtures talent and shapes future leaders. The institution provides more than 100 academic programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional streams, fostering experiential and collaborative learning. Students from across India and over 75 countries choose Acharya to gain cutting-edge knowledge, global perspectives, and the confidence to succeed in a rapidly evolving world.

Acharya's expansive 120-acre campus blends academic studios, cutting-edge laboratories, recreational spaces, and vibrant student life elements to create an inspiring setting for education and innovation. The institute also emphasizes industry engagement, international partnerships, and real-world application of skills, empowering students to translate ideas into impact.

About Acharya Institute of Technology

Acharya Institute of Technology (AIT), established in 2000 in Bengaluru, is one of the leading engineering and technology colleges in India. Affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University and accredited by NAAC and NBA, AIT is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and professional development.

AIT offers a wide array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, technology, science, and management, including B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and MCA. The institute emphasizes academic rigor, practical learning, industry collaboration, and research, preparing students to become capable professionals and innovators in their fields. With a strong faculty team and state-of-the-art infrastructure, AIT fosters a culture of critical thinking, creativity, and lifelong learning.

Students at AIT benefit from strong placement support, industry training, and opportunities to participate in hackathons, technical events, and global immersion programs, ensuring they are job-ready and future-focused.

About Mechatronics Engineering

Mechatronics engineering combines elements of mechanical engineering, electronics, computer science, and control engineering. The program prepares students for careers in designing and implementing intelligent systems and products. Our collaboration with Siemens helps students stay updated on current industry trends and are informed about the latest advancements and practices in the field.
