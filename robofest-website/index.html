ACHARYA ROBOFEST 2025

Where Innovation Meets Intelligent Machines

Hosted by Acharya Institute of Technology, Bengaluru

Events

Event 1: Robo-Triathlon

The ultimate robotics challenge combining three demanding tasks in one competition. Teams must design a versatile robot capable of precise manipulation, creative drawing, and intelligent color recognition—all within a strict 5-minute time limit.

Pick & Place

Design a gripper to pick cube-shaped objects and place them in designated areas with precision and speed.

Draw a Shape

Program your robot to draw geometric shapes (square, circle, rectangle, or triangle) on a plane sheet.

Robovision

Implement computer vision to identify and pick specific colored objects, placing them in designated boxes.

  • Each team consists of 2-4 members
  • Only 2 members allowed near the arena during the run
  • Maximum time to complete all 3 events: 5 minutes
  • A common robot must be used for all 3 events
  • No breaks between sub-events
  • Winner: Team completing all three events in shortest time
  • If no team completes all events, the team completing 2 events in shortest time wins
  • Any misconduct or track damage leads to immediate disqualification
Electrical & Power Requirements
  • Maximum voltage limit: 25.2V (6S LiPo equivalent)
  • Only LiPo or LiFePO4 batteries permitted
  • Batteries must be fully enclosed or secured in a fire-resistant compartment
Time Calculation

Total time is the sum of all three events. No specific time limit for individual events.

Event 2: Robo Race

A high-speed obstacle course challenge where custom-built robots navigate through ramps, speed breakers, sand traps, and sharp turns. Teams compete for the fastest lap time while avoiding penalties in this adrenaline-fueled race.

Robot Specs

Maximum dimensions: 300mm × 300mm × 300mm cube. Wheeled or tracked locomotion only. Custom-built chassis mandatory—no LEGO kits or toy cars allowed.

Track Features

Minimum 400mm lane width with obstacles including ramps (max 30° incline), speed breakers, sand traps, bridges, and zig-zag turns.

Race Format

Qualifiers: 1 lap. Finals: 2 laps or subject to reviewer call. Maximum allowed time: 4 minutes per run.

  • Each team consists of 2-4 members
  • Only 2 members allowed near the track during the run
  • Maximum voltage: 25.2V (6S LiPo equivalent)
  • Control method: Wired or Wireless (2.4 GHz RC)
  • Track surface: Plywood, carpet, or vinyl
  • Any misconduct or track damage leads to immediate disqualification
Penalties
  • Touching side wall: +5 seconds per touch
  • Skipping an obstacle: +20 seconds or restart
  • Pulling robot using wire: +10 seconds
  • Manual reset (Hand Touch): +10 seconds
Reset Rule

If the robot flips, stalls, or becomes stuck, driver must call "RESET!". Timer continues running. Robot is placed at the previous checkpoint with a +10 seconds penalty.

Scoring

Final Score = Base Time + Penalties (for teams who complete the track)

Event 3: Robo War

The ultimate combat robotics showdown. Teams design and build battle-ready robots equipped with spinners, flippers, and lifters to compete in intense 4-minute matches. Strategy, durability, and aggressive offense determine the champion.

Robot Limits

Maximum weight: 2.0 kg (including battery). Dimensions: 300mm × 300mm × 300mm starting box. Voltage limit: 25.2V (6S LiPo).

Weapons

Allowed: Spinners, lifters, flippers, clamps, wedges, pushers. Banned: Liquids, fire, nets, projectiles, EMP, high-voltage shocks.

Match Outcome

Win by Knockout (opponent immobile for 30 seconds), Judge's Decision (if time limit reached), or Tap Out (voluntary surrender).

Safety Requirements (Mandatory)
  • External kill switch accessible without lifting the robot
  • Must shut down both drive and weapon instantly
  • Fail-safe: Robot stops all functions if transmitter is turned off or signal is lost
  • Physical weapon lock/pin when outside the arena
  • Batteries fully enclosed, all wiring insulated and secured
Match Conduct
  • Match duration: 4 minutes
  • Drivers must remain behind the designated safety line
  • No physical contact with the arena during the match
  • Maximum pin time: 10 seconds (robot must release opponent)
Scoring System (100 Points Total)
  • Aggression (40 points): Actively attacking and maintaining offense
  • Damage (40 points): Actual damage or loss of opponent functionality
  • Control (20 points): Driving skill, stability, maneuvering, ring awareness
Disqualification

Participants may be disqualified for touching the arena during a match, damaging arena floor/walls/glass, using banned weapons, unsafe operation, or ignoring referee instructions.
