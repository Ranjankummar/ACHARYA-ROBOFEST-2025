About the Institution

About Acharya

Acharya is a premier educational community in Bengaluru with over three decades of excellence in higher education. With 100+ academic programs and students from 75+ countries, Acharya nurtures innovation, experiential learning, and global perspectives.

About Acharya Institute of Technology

Established in 2000, Acharya Institute of Technology (AIT) is a leading engineering institution affiliated with VTU and accredited by NAAC and NBA. AIT focuses on hands-on learning, industry collaboration, and research-driven education.

Event 1: Robo-Triathlon

A multi-skill robotics challenge that tests precision handling, creative motion control, and intelligent object recognition.

Pick & Place

Robot picks a cube-shaped object and places it accurately.

Draw a Shape

Robot draws a basic geometric shape on a sheet.

Robovision

Robot identifies a specific colored object and places it in the box.

Team Size: 2–4 Time Limit: 5 Minutes Max Voltage: 25.2V Battery: LiPo / LiFePO4
